安装前你需要知道什么

Sheaf 不要求注册账号，也不接管你的云盘。Markdown 文件仍然是普通的 .md 文件，可以继续用 Git、Finder、iCloud Drive、Dropbox 或任何同步工具管理。

AI 改写功能默认不会直接覆盖正文。Sheaf 会展示 diff，你确认后再应用；每次应用前也会保存版本快照，方便回滚。

Before you install

Sheaf does not require an account and does not move your writing into a proprietary database. Files stay as ordinary .md documents, so you can manage them with Finder, Git, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or any sync tool you trust.

AI rewriting does not overwrite your draft automatically. Sheaf shows a diff, lets you decide what to apply, and saves a version snapshot before each accepted edit.
