Sheaf vs Typora vs Obsidian

这三类 Markdown 工具解决的问题不同。Typora 擅长所见即所得写作，Obsidian 擅长知识库和双链，Sheaf 更关注“写好一篇稿子，并把它安全地改写、预览和导出”。These Markdown tools solve different problems. Typora is strong at WYSIWYG editing, Obsidian at knowledge bases and backlinks, while Sheaf focuses on finishing a publishable draft with safe AI review, preview, and export.

Sheaf 的重点：完成一篇可发布的稿子

Sheaf 的设计重心不是做一个庞大的知识库，而是帮助你把一篇文章写完、改好、预览清楚并导出到发布场景。它保留 Markdown 源文件，同时提供更接近成稿的衬线预览。

AI 改写：为什么 Sheaf 不直接覆盖正文

很多 AI 写作工具强调“一键改写”，但严肃写作需要可控。Sheaf 在应用 AI 修改前显示 diff，并保存版本快照。这样你可以把 AI 当成编辑助理，而不是把正文控制权交出去。

导出：Sheaf 面向发布场景

如果你写完 Markdown 后还要发布到公众号、博客、PDF 或社交平台，导出质量会直接影响效率。Sheaf 支持富文本 HTML、PDF、小红书卡片和长图导出，适合一篇内容多平台发布。

怎么选

  • 想要最轻的所见即所得 Markdown 编辑体验，可以看 Typora。
  • 想维护个人知识库、双链和插件工作流，可以看 Obsidian。
  • 想在本地写 Markdown，并用 AI 审阅式改写和多平台导出，可以试 Sheaf。

Sheaf focuses on finishing one publishable draft

Sheaf is not trying to become a huge knowledge base. It helps you write, review, preview, and export one piece of work while keeping the source as plain Markdown.

AI rewriting with review

Instead of one-click overwrites, Sheaf shows AI suggestions as diffs and saves version snapshots before applying accepted edits.

Publishing exports

When Markdown needs to become a blog post, newsletter, PDF, share card, or long image, export quality matters. Sheaf is built around that final publishing step.

How to choose

  • Choose Typora if you want a very light WYSIWYG Markdown editor.
  • Choose Obsidian if you want a personal knowledge base with backlinks and plugins.
  • Try Sheaf if you want local Markdown files, reviewed AI edits, and strong publishing exports.
